Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the academic and professional portfolio website of Dr. Sateesh Kumar Peddoju.

1. What information is collected?

We may collect basic contact information submitted via the contact form, including your name, email address, message subject, and message content solely to respond to your queries and academic communications.

2. How is your information used?

Your information is used strictly for academic collaboration, research inquiry response, and professional correspondence. We never sell, rent, or share personal contact data with third parties.

3. Data Protection and Security

We maintain standard security measures to protect submitted information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure, including SSL/TLS encrypted transmission.

4. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal information submitted to this website by contacting us directly.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy periodically. Any updates will be posted directly on this page with an updated revision date.
